Our Story

About Campus Bites

Built by students, for students — a smarter way to eat between classes.

Why we built this

Every student has waited too long for lunch

Campus Bites started with a simple observation: cafeteria lines eat into the ten minutes students actually have between classes. So we set out to redesign the whole experience — from the moment you decide what to eat, to the moment you pick it up.

Instead of one long line at 1 PM, Campus Bites spreads demand across the day with pre-ordering and pickup-slot balancing, and uses student voting to decide what actually gets cooked. The result is a cafeteria that runs on real data, not guesswork.
